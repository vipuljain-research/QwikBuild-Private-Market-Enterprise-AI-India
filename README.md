 QwikBuild — Private Market Research (Enterprise AI)
Research Note (PDF, 3 pages)
A buy-side style investment thesis on QwikBuild, a Bengaluru-based enterprise AI startup that raised a $2.3M pre-seed round led by Japan-listed Globalway in October 2026. The note tests whether AI-built software can become a defensible business, covering the business model, target customers, a ₹100 crore B2B trader ROI case, India market sizing, competition (Lovable, Emergent), key risks, and a Bear/Base/Bull valuation for 2030.
