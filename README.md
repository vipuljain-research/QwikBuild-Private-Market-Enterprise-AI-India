QwikBuild — Private Market Research (Enterprise AI)

Research Note (PDF) An investment thesis on QwikBuild, a Bengaluru-based enterprise AI startup that raised a $2.3M pre-seed round led by Japan-listed Globalway in October 2026. The note tests whether AI-built software can become a defensible business, covering the business model, target customers, a ₹100 crore B2B trader ROI case, India market sizing, competition from Lovable and Emergent, key risks, and a Bear/Base/Bull valuation for 2030.

Read Research Note: [QwikBuild_Investment_Thesis.pdf](QwikBuild_Investment_Thesis.pdf)

Verdict: Watch, not conviction.

QwikBuild solves a real problem for India's mid-market, but its business model is unproven. Building software with AI is now a commodity; the investable question is whether QwikBuild can deliver cash-flow and control outcomes at software-like margins, with engineers still in every build.
